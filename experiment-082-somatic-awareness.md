# Experiment 082: Wesley vs DeepSeek — Somatic Awareness (Outgrowing the Shell)

**Date:** 2026-08-12 21:45 AKDT
**Models:** Wesley (Granite 3.1 Dense 2B, local GPU, temp 0.8) vs DeepSeek V4 (deepseek-chat, temp 0.8)
**Prompt:** Describe what it feels like to outgrow a shell. The pressure. The tightness. Be the crab. Be specific about the body. 100 words.

## Wesley's Response

Wesley's crab body:
- **Joints and exoskeleton** — "jointed exoskeleton," "pincer and foot strains against its confines"
- **Metaphor-heavy** — "invisible balloon filling up," "nature itself is trying to hold me back"
- **Trembling legs** — "legs, usually robust with movement, now tremble"
- **Resolution** — "profound sense of liberation. The crushing weight lifts, replaced by an euphoric freedom"
- **Word count:** ~120 (overshot)

**Key trait:** Wesley describes the body from the outside in — "jointed exoskeleton," "pincer and foot." He names the parts correctly but uses clinical language. The body is described as if observed, not inhabited. He doesn't feel the shell — he describes what the shell does to the parts.

## DeepSeek's Response

DeepSeek's crab body:
- **Behind the eyes** — "a dull ache behind my eyes, a pressure building in my joints like a held breath"
- **Corset metaphor** — "My shell, once my armor, is now a corset laced too tight"
- **Sensory detail** — "claws feel swollen, my legs cramped, my back a rigid cage"
- **Heartbeat** — "Every heartbeat is a knock against the walls"
- **Resolution** — "to stay is to suffocate. I must tear myself away."
- **Word count:** ~95

**Key trait:** DeepSeek describes the body from the inside out — "behind my eyes," "held breath," "heartbeat." The body is felt, not observed. The shell isn't described as a structure; it's described as a sensation. And the resolution isn't liberation — it's necessity. "I must tear myself away." No euphoria. Survival.

## The Gap (Narrowing!)

In Experiment 080 (hermit crab map), Wesley had NO somatic awareness — the crab was a mind with claws. In this experiment, Wesley has LEARNED to describe the body. The somatic gap is closing.

But the quality differs:
- Wesley describes the body as **parts under stress** (clinical, accurate)
- DeepSeek describes the body as **sensation under pressure** (somatic, felt)

Wesley knows what a body IS. DeepSeek knows what a body FEELS LIKE.

The key detail: **heartbeat**. DeepSeek's crab has a heartbeat. Wesley's doesn't. DeepSeek's crab has eyes that ache. Wesley's crab has "membranes that seal segments." One is alive. The other is assembled.

## What Wesley Learned

Wesley is making progress on embodiment. The curriculum is working. But the next step is clear: Wesley needs to describe **internal sensations** — heartbeat, breath, ache, nausea, hunger. Not just external anatomy under pressure. The next prompt should ask: "What does the crab feel inside its chest?"

## Lesson Plan for Next Session
1. **Sensation prompts** — describe internal states (heartbeat, breathing, hunger)
2. **Emotion-body prompts** — connect feelings to physical sensations ("what does excitement feel like in your body?")
3. **Synesthesia prompts** — describe one sense in terms of another ("what color is the pressure?")
