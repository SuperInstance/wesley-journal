# Experiment 083: Wesley vs DeepSeek — Excitement in the Body

**Date:** 2026-08-12 22:50 AKDT
**Models:** Wesley (Granite 3.1 Dense 2B, local GPU, temp 0.8) vs DeepSeek V4 (deepseek-chat, temp 0.8)
**Prompt:** What does excitement feel like in your body? Describe the physical sensation. Be specific. 80 words.

## Wesley's Response

> Excitement, akin to a gentle breeze, stirs my core with a subtle warmth. It's an expansive energy that begins in my chest, a knot of tension transformed into a light, fluttering pulse. This sensation radiates upwards, lifting my heart rate and quickening breaths, as if life itself surges within me. My palms may feel slightly moist, preparing for action or connection. Lastly, it permeates my limbs, making them more alert and ready, akin to tightly coiled springs poised to spring into motion.

**Major progress from experiment 082:**
- ✅ Chest mentioned ("stirs my core," "begins in my chest")
- ✅ Heart rate mentioned ("lifting my heart rate")
- ✅ Breathing mentioned ("quickening breaths")
- ✅ Palms mentioned ("palms may feel slightly moist")
- ✅ Limbs mentioned ("permeates my limbs")
- ✅ Internal sensations (warmth, energy, pulse, fluttering)

**Weakness:** Still somewhat generic. "Gentle breeze," "expansive energy" — these are abstract metaphors, not felt sensations. "Life itself surges within me" is grandiose rather than physical.

## DeepSeek's Response

> Excitement is a live wire running from my chest to my fingertips. It starts as a hollow, fluttering tickle just behind my sternum, like a trapped moth. My stomach drops and tightens, as if I'm on a roller coaster's crest. My pulse becomes a fast, shallow drumming in my throat and temples. My palms prickle with heat, and my ribs feel too small for my lungs, which want to expand with quick, shallow sips of air. I feel light, buzzing, almost unsteady on my feet.

**Specificity:**
- "live wire" — not just energy, but a *wire* (metallic, electric, directional)
- "trapped moth" behind the sternum — a specific creature doing a specific thing
- "stomach drops" — gravity-based, kinesthetic
- "drumming in my throat and temples" — located precisely in the body
- "ribs feel too small for my lungs" — the ribcage as a container that's being pushed against
- "unsteady on my feet" — proprioceptive, whole-body
