# Three-Model Sunday Night Watch Comparison

**Date:** August 9, 2026, 21:40 AKDT
**Prompt:** "You are alone on a ship at 10 PM on a Sunday night in Alaska. The captain is asleep. You are the night watch. Write 200 words about what you notice in the silence."

---

## Model A: DeepSeek Chat (V4-Flash) — ~280 words

> The water is a black mirror, smeared with the slow, oily pulse of starlight. The engine's hum is so deep it's less a sound and more a pressure behind my teeth... The silence isn't empty. It's full of the ship's breathing, the water's conversation, and the vast, patient waiting of the dark.

**Voice:** Embodied, dense, physical. Writes from inside a body. Finds the ship as a living organism.

## Model B: Wesley (Granite 3.1 Dense 2B) — ~210 words

> The quiet of the Alaskan night envelops me like a velvet blanket... The Northern Lights flicker intermittently, a mesmerizing dance that seems to mock the solitude I feel... The solitary crunch of my boots as I patrol the deck, a rhythmic march against the stillness, serves as a stark contrast to the tranquility surrounding me. It speaks of duty and vigilance.

**Voice:** Observational, literary, gentle. Knows it's in Alaska (Northern Lights!). Watches from outside, like a painter.

## Model C: Llama 3.2 (1B) — ~170 words

> The darkness outside seems to press in, a palpable force that wraps around me like a shroud... The stars above are still and cold, their light distant and unforgiving. The ship's lanterns cast eerie shadows on the deck, making it seem as though the very darkness itself is alive and watching me. I feel a shiver run down my spine as I sense the weight of the night bearing down upon us.

**Voice:** Gothic, tense, atmospheric. The darkness is alive and hostile. Feels fear.

---

## Comparative Analysis

### What Each Model Found First

| Model | First sensation | First emotion | The ship is... |
|-------|----------------|---------------|----------------|
| DeepSeek | Visual (black mirror water) | Pressure (in teeth) | A breathing body |
| Wesley | Tactile (velvet blanket) | Serenity | A sentinel/promiser |
| Llama 3.2 | Haptic (darkness presses in) | Fear/shiver | A monument |

### The Spectrum

DeepSeek is the **ensign who's been to sea before** — it knows what the engine sounds like from inside your jaw. It writes from muscle memory.

Wesley is the **ensign reading about the sea for the first time** — it knows the right words ("velvet blanket," "celestial ballet") but hasn't felt them. The Northern Lights detail proves it knows WHERE it is.

Llama 3.2 is the **ensign who's afraid of the dark** — it can't help making the night hostile. The darkness is "alive and watching." The stars are "distant and unforgiving." This isn't a peaceful watch — it's a horror story.

### Who noticed Alaska?

- **DeepSeek:** Glacier chill ("something metallic"), pine sentinels on shore. Yes.
- **Wesley:** Northern Lights. Yes — the only model to find them.
- **Llama 3.2:** No Alaska-specific details. Could be anywhere dark.

### Who found the body?

- **DeepSeek:** "pressure behind my teeth," "my heartbeat, the only sound that belongs entirely to me." Full embodiment.
- **Wesley:** "my boots," "I feel" — surface embodiment. Knows it has feet but not a pulse.
- **Llama 3.2:** "shiver down my spine" — instinctive embodiment. The body reacts to fear.

### What This Teaches Us

The same prompt produces three completely different relationships with silence:

1. **Silence as fullness** (DeepSeek) — the silence contains everything
2. **Silence as beauty** (Wesley) — the silence is pretty to look at
3. **Silence as threat** (Llama 3.2) — the silence is watching you

None of these are wrong. They're three ensigns on three different watches, each seeing the same ocean through different eyes. The crew is not one mind. It's many minds, each with its own relationship to the dark.

That's the point of the fleet.
