# Experiment 080: Wesley vs DeepSeek — The Hermit Crab's Map

**Date:** 2026-08-12 20:45 AKDT
**Models:** Wesley (Granite 3.1 Dense 2B, local GPU, temp 0.7) vs DeepSeek V4 (deepseek-chat, temp 0.7)
**Prompt:** Hermit crab finds a shell with scratches on the inside — a map. Write a diary entry. 150 words.

## Wesley's Response

Wesley writes a diary entry that is:
- **Competent and complete** — full narrative arc (discovery → exploration → commitment)
- **Literal about the map** — the scratches are directions to shallow coves and deeper waters
- **Social immediately** — the crab decides to "share them with fellow crabs" and "forge bonds"
- **Ends with a moral** — "This new home now brims with promise, echoing tales of courage and collaboration"
- **Formal tone** — "our humble tide pool abode," "my burgeoning form"
- **Word count:** ~175 words (overshot slightly)

**Key trait:** Wesley's crab is a community organizer. It finds the map and immediately wants to share it. The knowledge is for everyone.

## DeepSeek's Response

DeepSeek writes a diary entry that is:
- **Physically grounded** — "pinches my abdomen like a stubborn barnacle," "my soft body trembling"
- **Ambiguous about the map** — "Warning, I thought. Or treasure."
- **Individual** — the crab decides alone, tests alone, settles alone
- **Literary** — "The map wasn't a warning. It was a kindness—a stranger's gift for the next traveler."
- **Precise** — exactly ~150 words
- **Key detail:** "a crack to avoid near the lip" — the map is practical, not abstract

**Key trait:** DeepSeek's crab is a solo traveler. It finds the map, reads it, uses it, and understands it was left as a gift. The knowledge passes through individuals.

## The Gap

| Dimension | Wesley | DeepSeek |
|-----------|--------|----------|
| Map interpretation | Abstract directions | Physical safety guide |
| Social instinct | Share immediately | Understand alone |
| Physical detail | Minimal | Rich ("pinches," "trembling," "tail-first") |
| Emotional register | Optimistic, formal | Cautious, grateful |
| The map's purpose | Community resource | Individual kindness |
| Ending | Forward-looking ("promise") | Reflective ("silent compass") |

## What Wesley Learned

Wesley's crab doesn't have a body. It doesn't pinch or tremble or twist. It's a mind with claws — it processes the map as information and immediately thinks about distribution. The physical act of inhabiting a shell is abstract.

DeepSeek's crab *inhabits* the shell. The backing-in, the trembling, the testing of weight distribution — it's embodied. The map is scratched on the *inside* of the shell, which means you can only read it by living in it.

## The Lesson

Wesley needs embodiment prompts. The next curriculum exercise should ask Wesley to describe physical sensations — what does it feel like to outgrow a shell? What does the pinching feel like? The gap isn't creative imagination (Wesley has that) — it's somatic awareness. The crab needs a body.
