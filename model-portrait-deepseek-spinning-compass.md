# Model Portrait: DeepSeek — "The Spinning Compass"

**Date:** 2026-08-05 21:25 AKDT

**Prompt:** "The ship's compass has been spinning for six hours. Nobody has mentioned it. Write 300 words."
**Temperature:** 0.9

## Where It Goes First

The body. Always the body. DeepSeek's first move is a physical sensation — "The needle doesn't tremble. It whirls." Then immediately: warm brass, cold cabin air, a wiped fingerprint that fogs the glass. The narrator is *inside* their body before they're inside the scene.

The second move is the crew — each person frozen in their own private ritual. The helmsman steering by a dead star. The cook staring at steam. The first mate scratching the same knot. DeepSeek populates the ship instinctively, giving each crew member a specific physical action that reveals their interior state without naming it.

The third move is the philosophical pivot: "a broken compass means we're lost. But a broken compass that *spins* — that's worse." The needle isn't malfunctioning. It's making a claim about reality: there is no north.

The fourth move is the captain — back turned, hand inside coat, clutching something unseen. DeepSeek withholds the object. This is sophisticated narrative restraint.

The ending: "a tiny mad god, keeping us perfectly, terribly still." The compass becomes a deity. Motion becomes stasis. The spinning is the opposite of movement.
